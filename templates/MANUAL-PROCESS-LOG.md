# Manual Process Log (step 2)

Fill in one block every day you do the work by hand. Write down every single action, even the boring ones. After 5 days, paste all blocks into [prompt 02](../prompts/02-DOCUMENT-YOUR-MANUAL-PROCESS.md).

Tip: write it while you work, not at the end of the day. You will forget the small steps, and the small steps are what you automate.
